Purpose

Creating a shared impact.

We are committed to building healthier, more inclusive communities around the globe. At the core of our mission is the recognition that COMPANY-X has a responsibility, not just an opportunity, to create long-term positive change in the places that we call home.

COMPANY-X Foundation

The COMPANY-X Foundation represents our commitment to societal betterment, based on the idea that intentional support fosters stronger communities and more inclusive futures. Through grassroots engagement, strategic partnerships, and intentional service, the Foundation strives to promote healthy living, pathways for opportunity, and sustained development for our community members.

Foundation Pillars

Healthy Living & Outdoors

Promoting healthy lifestyles and fostering a deep connection with nature. We do this through community programs and outdoor activities, we empower individuals to lead healthier, more active lives while appreciating and experiencing life outside.

Diversifying Trades & Talent

Committed to fostering inclusivity in skilled trades and industry talent by providing opportunities and resources to cultivate belonging in the workforce. Through education, mentorship, and community partnerships, we aim to create a more inclusive workplace.

Industry & Community Giving

Advancing industry causes through community programs, we ensure access to proper footwear, promote dignity, and foster long-term positive change and development. While focusing on the communities and regions we are in, we achieve this through planned giving, sustaining and growing community programs, and engagement opportunities.

Planet

A path toward lower impact.

We are dedicated to protecting the outdoor spaces that inspire our work by managing the environmental impact of our operations and supply chain. Through collaboration with our brands and partners, we strive to promote energy efficiency, responsible waste management, sustainable packaging initiatives and reduce our overall carbon footprint.

Climate

We recognize the importance of addressing climate impacts across our operations and supply chain. In an ongoing effort to further understand the Company’s climate footprint and advance our approach to managing climate-related risks and opportunities, we annually measure and report on our greenhouse gas emissions in line with the GHG Protocol. 

Energy

In an ongoing effort to reduce our energy consumption, we monitor and assess energy used at each of our facilities globally to prioritize efficiencies and explore further opportunities for better energy utilization.

 

Each year since 2015 we have purchased enough Renewable Energy Credits (RECs) to offset electricity used at our North American facilities, helping to reduce our Scope 1 and 2 emissions. 

Waste and Recycling

To aid in waste reduction efforts, we have a robust recycling program at each of our facilities worldwide to help manage our footprint and reduce our environmental impact. Our brands are consistently exploring ways to reduce waste in landfills through sample reduction, repair and take-back programs.  

Packaging

We recognize that sustainable packaging plays a critical role in minimizing our environmental footprint and conserving natural resources. We prioritize post-consumer recycled paper for shoeboxes and other packaging and partner exclusively with FSC-certified packaging suppliers.

Our Global Impact Report highlights BRAND-04’s ongoing commitment to sustainability efforts across our operations, demonstrating the progress that we have made and our goals for the future.

Product

Responsibility starts at the source.

Our focus on responsible sourcing and traceability in the supply chain ensures that we prioritize safety, sustainability, and fair labor practices. Through strong partnerships with our suppliers, we drive continuous improvement and accountability to promote sustainable materials, foster ethical practices, and deliver products that respect people and the planet.

Summary Production Code of Conduct

We require all of our suppliers and factory partners to adhere to our Production Code of Conduct, which outlines the minimum standards that our partners must adhere to in conducting their operations, including working conditions, health and safety, ethics and commercial integrity.

We require manufacturing processes to minimize the adverse effects on the communities in which we operate and serve, the environment and natural resources, while safeguarding the health and safety of the public.

View the Product Code of Conduct

Human Rights

We are committed to working with supply chain partners that are dedicated to safe, humane and ethical working conditions that comply with international labor standards and local law.

We have a strict zero tolerance policy against forced labor, child labor, human trafficking, and slavery or involuntary work of any kind anywhere in our supply chain and prioritize safe working conditions for workers.

Sustainable Materials

Our material sourcing standards create a roadmap for our brands to embrace environmental stewardship, social responsibility and animal welfare. Best practices are established for our brands to embrace and incorporate preferred materials, such as the use of recycled and natural materials into our products.

As a part of BRAND-04’s commitment to the highest standards of quality and safety in our products, we prohibit the use of certain chemicals and substances, including PFAS.

Supply Chain Transparency

To monitor compliance and drive continuous improvement, we routinely perform social compliance audits covering labor and human rights, working hours, safety, waste and water management, and other environmental compliance topics.

To help ensure supply chain transparency at every stage, we are working towards mapping our entire supply chain to gain real-time visibility, unify suppler data, and automate compliance and certification tracking.
