Who We Are

Good products for good people.

Founded in 1883 on the simple desire to create good products for good people, Wolverine Worldwide has grown into one of the world's leading marketers and licensors of branded casual, active lifestyle, work, outdoor sport, athletic, children's and uniform footwear and apparel — reaching consumers in approximately 170 countries and territories.

  • Headquartered in Rockford, Michigan, USA
  • Listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: WWW)
  • Named 2025 Company of the Year by Footwear News
