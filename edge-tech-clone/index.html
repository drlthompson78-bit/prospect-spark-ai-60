Why we exist

Buildings drive nearly 40% of global carbon emissions. We exist to change that.

Edge creates buildings that produce more than they take: offices that generate their own energy, learn from their users and get measurably better over time.

From Amsterdam's Zuidas to the South Bank of London, we combine architecture, data and sustainable engineering to raise the standard of what a building can be.

Impact

Paris Proof,
by design.

We believe no commercial building should consume more than 70 kWh per square metre per year. Every Edge building is engineered to meet that bar — and to keep improving after opening day.

01

Energy positive where possible

Solar façades, thermal storage and smart load balancing let our best buildings generate more energy than they consume.

02

Healthy by default

Air quality, daylight and acoustics are continuously measured and tuned — WELL certification is the baseline, not the ambition.

03

Circular materials

Timber-hybrid structures and demountable interiors reduce embodied carbon and keep materials in use for the long run.
